What this is, honestly

No LLM is involved anywhere on this page. The answer is the best-matching passage, quoted. The trace evals are plain rules over a sample trace you can edit, not a connection to the real AgentWatch. Retrieval is BM25 with light stemming, and confidence is the top score times the square of the share of question terms (weighted by rarity) found in that passage. The three evals shown beside each trace are rule-based, in the spirit of AgentWatch. The refuse-unless-supported behavior is the same idea I trained into a model in RAFT ArXiv.

How it was tuned, and what the scores mean. I wrote 432 questions from my résumé (paraphrases, recruiter-style phrasing, one-word queries, and things the résumé does not say). Settings and per-passage learned aliases were chosen by 5-fold cross-validation on 302 of them. On the 60 held-out test questions it answers 37/43 correctly and refuses 15/17 out-of-scope ones. On 70 fresh questions written after the model was frozen: 41/48 and 17/22, about 83%. Train and dev scores are higher because it saw them, so ignore those. It still makes mistakes: its weak spot is confidently answering a question that only sounds related, such as asking about an employer he never worked for. Your own questions are the real test.
